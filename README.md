# [TrafficSignal]: AI for [Traffic safety concerns in San Jose.]

Client: City of San José, [Speeding, school zones, and potholes on residental streets]
Team: [Nancy Kherella, Sofia Amir, Russel Jonathan Estrada, Arvin Vinluan, Ahmed Al-Tameemi], Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I built the report history section and adjusted the app's UX design.

## The problem
San Jose residents don't feel safe on the streets at times due to speeding, dangerous school zones and potholes. The current reporting process can be confusing and time-consuming, making it difficult for residents to communicate their concerns.

## What we built
- The prototype provides an organized and easy way to report safety concerns
- The user sees a simple and user-friendly app to communicate with the City of San Jose
- A human still decides whether an error has occurred on the user's end or if a report needs immediate attention


## What I would do next
What I would do next is have San Jose residents test the app's usability to gain feedback from real potential users.
